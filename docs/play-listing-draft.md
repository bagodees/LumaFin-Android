## Short description (80 characters max)

Your Jellyfin library on your phone, foldable and tablet, in the LumaFin style.

## Full description

LumaFin brings your Jellyfin server to your phone and tablet with the same look as LumaFin on your TV.

- Home with Continue Watching, Next Up and what's new
- Library grids sorted by name, date added, release or Top Rated
- Title pages with seasons, episodes, cast and similar titles
- A player with audio and subtitle choices that picks up where you left off on any device
- Made for every screen: phones, tablets, and foldables folded, unfolded or half-folded on a table
- Optional Seerr requests through the SeerrFin plugin on your server

LumaFin is an independent app built on the open-source Jellyfin project. You need your own Jellyfin server.
